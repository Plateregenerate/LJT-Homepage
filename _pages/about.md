I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), working with Professor Junxian He in the HKUST NLP Group. My research lies at the intersection of natural language processing and machine learning, and focuses on LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models, and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

## About

I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024 and joined the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology as a Ph.D. student in the same year. Before starting my doctoral studies, I spent time on industrial and laboratory research internships at Shanghai AI Lab, Tencent WXG and MINIMAX, where I worked on foundation model evaluation, vision-language model understanding and large language model reasoning. My first-author works study verifiable logical reasoning data, the perception bottleneck of vision-language models on chart understanding, and the universal truthfulness structure inside large language models.
